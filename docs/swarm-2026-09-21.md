## Hypothesis and observed causes

**Hypothesis:** the 100-column capture needs reflow rather than whole-screen
scaling to remain readable in portrait. Inspection confirmed five command/output
blocks, a wide comparison table, and the existing disclosure only on landscape
GIFs. No portrait video/poster or video-format/all-frame gate existed.

The fix reads the existing cast, preserves its recorded outputs, removes only
terminal controls and trailing accessibility coordinates, stacks table fields,
and wraps prose at 41 characters. It creates five eight-second cards with 38 px
body text and a fixed 280 px disclosure header (36 px text). Titles are editorial;
the footer explicitly says **textual replay, fixture data, edited timing, silent
draft, not a new phone execution**. This is not a pixel-for-pixel screen recording.

During reproduction, concurrent Inkscape processes intermittently aborted with
`Gio::DBus::Error`, even with per-card app IDs. The renderer now exports the five
cards in **one Inkscape batch with a process-specific app ID**. Both portable and
VAAPI generation plus validation passed after that change. No shared service was
restarted. At takeover no R4 Nix dependency fetch remained; unrelated workers'
fetches/builds were left alone. ShellCheck was absent from PATH but an installed
0.11.0 store binary was found and used, without fetching or reinstalling it.
